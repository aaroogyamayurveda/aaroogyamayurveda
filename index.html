Ortho Gold Oil

Joint-care Oil

120 ml external-use oil. The product label describes its use with gentle massage over the affected area.

Label-described benefits

  • Relieves joint & muscular pain
  • Controls swelling & inflammation of joints
  • Reduces stiffness and tension
  • Strengthens bones and muscles

Directions

Apply a small amount to the affected area and massage gently until absorbed. Use 2–3 times daily or as directed by an Ayurvedic physician.

Caution: For external use only. Do not apply on cuts, wounds or broken skin.

Ortho Gold Capsules

Joint-care Capsules

60 capsules. The label describes the product as supporting joint health, reducing pain and inflammation, and improving mobility.

Key ingredients per capsule

• Shallaki • Ashwagandha • Nirgundi • Rasna • Haridra • Saunth • Satavari • Suddh Guggul • Hadjod, along with the other ingredients listed on the product label.

Directions

Take 1–2 capsules twice a day.

Caution: Do not exceed the recommended dosage. Consult a healthcare professional if pregnant or under medication. Keep out of reach of children.
